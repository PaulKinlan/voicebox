# Voicebox interface exploration

Operate / Experience. A browser surface for conversation while things are made; desktop work and phone return visits. The production stack remains undecided. All study content and authority state are synthetic; microphone and filesystem access are absent.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Assets arrive around the conversation and remain when the words have passed. No transcript-first dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit isocan Voice: Inter, charcoal or off-white ground, blue microphone, fine rings, restrained green activity, ordinary rounded controls.

STORY: Watch a note, a small page and a tool appear; interrupt speech without cancelling work; inspect a concrete decision when needed.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Quiet project identity above an open artefact field; one large page, a note and a smaller tool. A centred caption and microphone anchor the lower third. Logs and settings stay peripheral. Arrivals settle into stable places without moving focus.

FORM: Brief-pinned isocan Voice (N12), extended with assets. No world-selection seed: the user's explicit reference overrides a random direction. Three interaction models share this language.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Open decisions

Studio, Beside and Return are alternative attention models, not final approved product modes. Actual host events, security, admission, OPFS and audio are owned by the environment/harness implementation. The study must not impersonate those implementations.
