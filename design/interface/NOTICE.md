# Sources and demonstration material

Voicebox's interface study was authored for this repository. Its microphone treatment, palette and separation of captions from Logs/Settings adapt the visual language of [PaulKinlan/isocan](https://github.com/PaulKinlan/isocan), inspected at `5e7f7bbcaabe0c15a1e7a171e938eabf07782f54`. These are modified, self-contained study files; no isocan runtime or credentials are included. A copy of isocan's [Apache 2.0 license](licenses/isocan-Apache-2.0.txt) is included.

The unchanged Inter Latin font was copied from isocan's `packages/voice-agent/public/fonts/inter-latin.woff2`. It is by the Inter Project Authors and is distributed with its [SIL Open Font License](fonts/Inter-OFL.txt).

The Fieldnotes page, On noticing note, little sorter, package sources, identities, activity and permissions are synthetic examples authored for this study. They contain no personal journal content and are not real execution receipts. No generated or stock images are used. Screenshots are browser captures of this HTML/CSS, with provenance in the evidence receipt, not design comps.
